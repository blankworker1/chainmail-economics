# Chainmail Economics: version 2 design

**Status: draft. Nothing here is built or tested with a class.** Version 1 is the working game described in `README.md`. This file records the design agreed so far for version 2, and the questions still open.

## What version 2 adds

Version 1 plays out five of the seven aspects: owning, exchanging, giving, pricing, and no debt. Version 2 adds the other two, producing and saving, and uses them to deliver the core message of the model:

> No economic model is neutral. Every model has settings that shape the outcome before anyone acts. Chainmail Economics shows its settings.

The class plays the same game at three levels: Level 1, Level 2, and Level 3. Each level runs on a different hidden setting. The settings are revealed in the debrief.

## Two words

- **Units** are what a link holds: 3 units of food, 4 units of skills. Version 1 calls these levels.
- **Levels** are the three stages of play: Level 1, Level 2, and Level 3.

## What stays the same

Everything in version 1 stays: the cloth, the bonds, the four-bond limit, the gift of 600 sats, trading along bonded routes, the wallet as ledger, shocks, and the closing bell.

## Using

Every link uses up **one unit of energy and one unit of food** each period.

- Use runs on the countdown clock, not on rounds. In a 15-minute level there are three uses, one every 5 minutes.
- Use applies to every link, bonded or not.
- Use is not rolled. It happens to everyone.

Enough stays at 3 units, which now means three periods of reserve.

## Working

Work is decided by two dice, rolled on the board for everyone at once.

- **A two-sided dice** picks what this roll produces: energy or food.
- **A six-sided dice** sets the threshold. Every link whose units of skills are at or above the roll gains one unit.

Three limits apply:

- **Only bonded links work.** A link with no bonds produces nothing.
- **A link at zero cannot work.** A link with no energy or no food produces nothing until it has both again.
- **Land caps food.** A link cannot hold more units of food than it has units of land.

## Land and money

Land and money are not produced and not used up.

- The total amount of land is set by the land rolls at the start of the game.
- The total amount of money is set by the gift.
- Both move freely between links through trade.
- Money stays fixed even when the economy changes.

A link short of land must buy some before it can hold enough food.

## Running out

A link at zero has to ask for help. Asking is a skill, and so is noticing who needs help. The game sets no rule for this. Whether giving becomes normal is left to the class, and is a subject for the debrief.

## Saving

Saving has no rule. It is what people do in a healthy economy, and it is the **hidden behavioural variable** of the game. Players are not told to save or scored on it. The debrief shows who did.

Saving can take three forms, all of which arise from the rules above:

- **Goods:** units held above 3 carry a link through a bad roll or a shock.
- **Sats:** sats kept at one level are there to spend at the next.
- **Capacity:** sats spent on skills or land raise what a link can produce or hold.

## The hidden setting

The setting is the number of work rolls for each use. It decides how much a period of work yields against what a period of living costs. It is the only thing that changes between levels. Everything else on the board acts the same.

| Level | Hidden setting | Work rolls per use | Roll interval in 15 minutes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Level 1 | Abundant | 7 | About every 43 seconds |
| Level 2 | Balanced | 5 | Every 60 seconds |
| Level 3 | Scarce | 3 | Every 100 seconds |

The class is unlikely to notice that the dice are rolled less often. If someone does, it is not a problem: the facilitator frames it as levelling up.

### What a simulation shows

Each row is 2,000 simulated plays of 24 bonded links, with three uses and the rules above. "Enough" counts energy and food only.

| Level | Links with enough, no trading | Links with enough, if shared evenly | Links that ran out at some point | Stock at end against start |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Level 1 | 43% | 79% | 20% | 1.25 |
| Level 2 | 35% | 71% | 26% | 0.98 |
| Level 3 | 22% | 57% | 38% | 0.69 |

- Break-even is about five rolls per use. Below that the cloth as a whole loses stock.
- The gap between the first two columns is what trading and giving can close.
- At Level 3, even perfect sharing leaves over 40% of links short.

The simulation leaves out trading, shocks, and links with no bonds. It shows the direction, not final numbers.

## The session

The class plays three levels of 15 minutes each.

- **Order:** Level 1 gives the most students the best chance of a good outcome. Each level after it is harder and less equal.
- **Hidden:** the class knows it is moving up a level. It is not told what changes until the debrief.
- **Same cloth:** all three levels use the same cloth and the same starting cards.
- **Goods reset:** energy and food return to their starting values between levels.
- **Sats carry over:** wallets are not reset. What a player holds at the end of one level is what they start the next with.
- **No debrief between levels:** only a short pause while the board resets.

The game is designed to run at a slow pace. The facilitator gives the class one tip before play starts: **always think before you act.**

## The debrief

One debrief follows the closing bell of Level 3. The screen shows the three levels side by side:

- links with enough at the closing bell
- links that ran out at some point
- average price of energy and of food
- gifts made
- sats held by each link at the end of each level

Then the settings are revealed. The factors that shaped the outcome are discussed by who controls them:

| Factor | Set by | What it stands for |
|---|---|---|
| Work rolls per use | The facilitator, hidden | The rules of the system |
| Order of the three settings | The facilitator, hidden | The path the system takes |
| How often shocks come | The facilitator, hidden | The stability of the system |
| Starting rolls | Chance | What each person begins with |
| How many links are bonded | The players | How people choose to organise |
| Saving | Each player | How people choose to act over time |

Questions to start with:

- Did the three levels feel different? When did you notice?
- Did prices change? The amount of money did not.
- Who had sats left for Level 3, and why?
- Who ran out, and what happened next?
- Which of these factors could you change, and which were set for you?

## Open questions

1. **How phones keep in step.** The card page has no link to the board. Proposed: the board sounds a tone at each use and each work roll, and players tap "Use" or "Work" on their phone.
2. **Who a gift can reach.** Today a gift needs a bonded route and counts as the giver's one purchase for the round. A link with no bonds could then never be helped. Proposed: gifts ignore both limits.
3. **Buying once per round.** With work and use on the clock, rounds no longer mark anything in the market. Proposed: each link may buy once between work rolls.
4. **Skills.** Selling skills does not deplete the seller, so the total grows through trade. This makes skills the natural investment. It needs testing for balance.
5. **Enough.** Enough is 3 units each of energy, food, land, and skills. Land and skills only change by trade, so some links may need to buy both before they can reach it.
6. **Shocks.** Whether the shock rate is the same at all three levels. If the dice roll frequency is to be the only variable, it should be.
7. **Bonds between levels.** Whether "the same cloth" means bonds are restored to their starting state, or carry over as sats do.

## What needs building

- Use on the clock, with a tone.
- The two work dice on the board, on a timer set by the level.
- The land cap on food, and the rule that a link at zero cannot work.
- The three-level sequence, with the hidden setting for each level and a reset of goods between levels.
- "Use" and "Work" buttons on the card page.
- The debrief screen with data from all three levels.
- A fuller simulation that includes trading, giving, shocks, and sats carried between levels.
