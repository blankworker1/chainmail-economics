Username, then five digits in the order E F L M S. Energy, food, land, and skills are dice rolls from 1 to 6. The Money digit is ignored, since money is set by the game. A message sent from the card page can be pasted into either box.

One trade per line: buyer, seller, attribute, price. For example F6 D5 F 120.
